Slides

A presentation is a folder: every file or subfolder is one slide, in name order.
Pictures and videos fill the screen, .md files become text slides,
a folder puts its .md text next to its pictures. <!-- comments --> are speaker notes.

⚠️ This browser can't open folders. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Drag cards to reorder (files get renamed to 001-…, 002-…). Drop files here to add them as slides.
