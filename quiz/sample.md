# Sample quiz

Slides are separated by a line with `---`.

A slide with a task list is a **question**. Everything else is a plain slide.

---

## What is the capital of Australia?

- [ ] Sydney
- [x] Canberra
- [ ] Melbourne
- [ ] Perth

Canberra was purpose-built as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne.

---

# Half-time

Text after the options of a question (like the Canberra note) is shown only after the reveal.

`<!-- time: 30 -->` overrides the time limit for one question. `time: 0` means no limit.

---
